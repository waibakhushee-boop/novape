What is Vaping?

Vaping is the act of inhaling and exhaling an aerosol produced by an electronic cigarette or similar device. These devices heat a liquid (commonly called e-liquid or vape juice) which usually contains nicotine, flavouring, and other chemicals.

Even though vaping is often marketed as a safer alternative to smoking, it still exposes users to addictive nicotine and potentially harmful chemicals.
